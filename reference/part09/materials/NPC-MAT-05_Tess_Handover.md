# NPC-MAT-05：不把意愿写成完成
类型：三方交接脚本。目标：IELTS Academic 6.5；未校准，未配音。
场景：R6-S08；CQ-TESS-03。只说明本地通知链，不新增六方协议或终局条件。

**Finn:** The local team has accepted the next watch. The equipment is on the delivery record, but one handover has not been confirmed. Those are two separate facts.

**Tess:** Then do not send a message saying that everything is ready. Tell the receiving team what has arrived, what still needs checking, and who can answer if something is missing.

**Player:** We can keep the original contact and add a second person. Does the receiving team agree to that arrangement?

**Finn:** They have agreed to the second contact, provided both people can see the same current record. I have their reply here.

**Tess:** Good. I may still disagree with the decision about the wider network. That is not a reason to leave this handover unfinished. People will need a working arrangement under any of the plans we are considering.

**Player:** I will confirm the local message separately from the final operating agreement.

**Finn:** That is the right distinction. This reply closes a local communication task; it does not replace a trial, deliver another crate, or sign on behalf of all six regions.

制作说明：角色的政治分歧不撤销已履行义务；设备、试验、协议依旧读取真实回执。

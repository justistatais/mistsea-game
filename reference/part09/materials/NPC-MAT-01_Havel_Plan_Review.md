# NPC-MAT-01：哈文核对行动
类型：对话脚本。目标：IELTS Academic 6.5；未校准，未配音。
场景：R1-S03或R1-S04的合法通信入口。条件：玩家已经提交当前撤离提案；相关设备材料可见。
用途：回应已提出的意图，不在首次接受任务时自动给出全部答案。任何方案、字段或设备配置改变后按QEV重新核对。
允许影响：形成待玩家确认的行动摘要；不得仅因对白播放而离港。

**Havel:** You have explained which route you want to use. Before I pass the instruction to the crew, I need to check that we mean the same thing by “ready”.

**Player:** Which part is still unclear?

**Havel:** The spare light can work, but that is not the same as having it fitted and tested on this ferry. Your note says the crew can leave after the bracket is replaced. Does that also include connecting the onboard battery and checking the complete arrangement?

**Player:** Yes. They should complete those steps before departure, and repeat the check if a connection changes.

**Havel:** That gives them a clear sequence. I will keep the main generator out of this plan. The inspection team has not yet confirmed that it is safe to use.

**Player:** Please show me the final instruction before you send it.

**Havel:** Of course. Nothing leaves this desk until you have confirmed the actual arrangement. A clear plan is useful; a plan that describes work as finished when it is not would cause a different problem.

制作说明：玩家台词为可接受意图示例，不要求照抄。实际收尾由Q-102设备、提案与提交规则执行。

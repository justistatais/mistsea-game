# NPC-MAT-02：莉娜选择怎样联系家人
类型：对话脚本。目标：IELTS Academic 6.5；未校准，未配音。
场景：R6-S02或双方许可的HUB-S01中继。条件：CQ-LINA-02与本人回信核验完成；CQ-LINA-03正在进行。
隐私：回信不自动向同伴全队公开；不在本脚本加入具体住址。

**Lina:** I used to think that finding him would settle everything. It would at least tell me whether I should keep looking. Now there is a reply, and I am not sure what to ask first.

**Player:** He has agreed to exchange letters. He has not agreed to a visit yet.

**Lina:** I know. I do not want you to turn a small permission into a promise on his behalf. Please tell him that I received his message. He does not have to explain all those years in one letter.

**Player:** Would you like to suggest meeting, or leave that for another reply?

**Lina:** Suggest it as a possibility, not an arrangement. I would like to see him, but he has a life there. If he would rather continue writing for now, send me the answer as it is.

制作说明：此为“希望询问见面可能性”的一条分支；其他可接受分支为继续通信或双方明确暂缓。不得将同意通信投影为同意见面。见面实际安排仍检查双方当前许可，不靠语气评分。

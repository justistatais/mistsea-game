# NPC-MAT-06：甲板上的椅子
类型：生活对话。目标语境：6.5项目；日常句可自然简短，未校准，未配音。
场景：HUB-S01；莉娜、奥伦已经招募且同时可用。无额外奖励，不携带主线秘密。

**Lina:** I thought you fixed that chair yesterday.

**Oren:** I did. Today I am making it quieter.

**Lina:** It was perfectly quiet while nobody was sitting on it.

**Oren:** That was not the condition I was testing.

**Lina:** Then write a warning: safe for people who never sit down.

**Oren:** Or you could hold this part while I tighten the other side.

**Lina:** All right. But when it is finished, we are allowed to use it. You cannot keep every repaired object as an unfinished experiment.

**Oren:** Agreed. Sit down after I put the last screw back.

制作说明：一次安全节点展示；可以暂停。人物实际占用由QEV统一调度，不能因这段小场景挪走正在战斗或施工的人。

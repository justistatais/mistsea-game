# NPC-MAT-03：修复不是免除责任
类型：三方对话脚本。目标：IELTS Academic 6.5；未校准，未配音。
场景：R3-S03；CQ-OREN-02辅助警报器已实际修复，独立复核尚待完成。
不改变：Q-302的签章事实、Q-304的主线结果。

**Oren:** The signal now reaches both rooms. I replaced the damaged connection, and the spare parts are listed here. Someone other than me should test it before the residents rely on it.

**Pera:** That is a useful repair. It does not answer why the earlier equipment was accepted with the safety check incomplete.

**Oren:** I am not asking it to. I signed that record, and I should have refused. I can explain the pressure I was under, but that does not make the signature belong to someone else.

**Player:** What can be agreed today without closing the wider review?

**Pera:** We can appoint a reviewer for this repair and make the result available to the residents. His statement about the earlier check must remain separate. I will not write that everyone has forgiven him.

**Oren:** Then record the repair and the remaining questions separately. I can also train someone to repeat the test. I will not always be here to do it.

制作说明：修复件来自本支线工坊包，不挪用六塔主物资。玩家不需要说服佩拉“原谅”才能完成安全复核。此处不复制主线验收奖励。

# COM-MAT-02：潮门塔检修程序

用途：Q-104本地虚构装置规则。原创6.5目标制作稿，未经外部标定。不是现实电气维修指南。

Both cooling valves must be closed before the local supply can be isolated. The order in which the valves are closed does not matter. However, isolating the supply is not a substitute for closing either valve.

The two valve controls are positioned at the front of the maintenance area. The isolation control is at the rear. A qualified member of the crew may operate a control after reaching the corresponding working position. No particular tool model is required for the normal procedure.

Once both valves are closed, the protective lock around the unit will release. The crew can then bring the unit under control. If the isolation step has not been completed, the system will remain in a holding state rather than register a safe shutdown.

Check the displayed positions instead of relying on an earlier confirmation. Changing a relevant connection makes the previous check insufficient. Ordinary protective equipment can reduce the risk to the crew, but it cannot change the valve settings or certify the installation.

Take time to read the current warnings. The inspection record is not a test of how quickly a message can be read.

答案边界：两阀可交换；隔离必须在两阀之后；净化/盾/暴露不代替设备动作；一切为本游戏受控虚构条件。

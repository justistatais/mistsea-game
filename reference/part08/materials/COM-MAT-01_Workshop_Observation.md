# COM-MAT-01：西工坊观察记录

用途：既有工坊材料的可选现场补充，不覆盖Q-102已定正文。版本0.1，原创6.5目标制作稿，尚无试测。

The maintenance unit is still operating from its own reserve. Switching off the harbour generator will therefore not stop it. Its movements follow a short inspection cycle, but the direction of each attack should be checked before anyone enters the marked area.

An arm strike follows the person selected by the unit. Moving to another position does not, by itself, remove that person from danger. A scanning sweep is different: it covers the indicated section of the floor. Anyone outside that section is clear of the sweep, provided that no other warning applies.

Repeated pressure may interrupt the unit, although it needs time to recover before the same method can work again. Do not assume that every hit will cancel an attack. A prepared device is useful only when the unit actually starts the action for which the device was set.

The replacement bracket remains on the inner shelf. Stopping the unit creates access; it does not collect the part or repair the ferry. The fixed inspection stand and the ferry's navigation beacon are separate pieces of equipment.

# COM-MAT-03：中央机组交接录音脚本

状态：仅脚本；双人；建议作为暂停可重播的现场材料。原创6.5目标稿，实际语速/音质及任务负担需另测，无已完成音轨。

Technician: Rook no longer controls the command layer, but that does not mean the maintenance programme has stopped. The relays can restart it without another order from him.

Inspector: Then the first step is to isolate the two local links and verify their present configuration?

Technician: Correct. Either link can be dealt with first. Do not proceed on the basis of an old check if somebody has changed the connections.

Inspector: What happens after the unit reaches the first holding point?

Technician: We can address the automatic restart. Both relay assemblies have to be inactive before the bypass can be latched. Reaching the holding point alone does not prove that the relays are inactive.

Inspector: And the final stop?

Technician: Set the damping arrangement, verify it, and then authorise the controlled stop. Authorisation applies to that checked arrangement. It does not give us permission to alter the connections and keep using the same record.

Inspector: Understood. We will report that the central programme is stable once those steps are complete. We will not describe the entire network as converted.

Technician: Exactly. The regional preparations and the final operating decision remain separate matters.
